

# Top
The goal to stopping the deep half guard is getting an under hook. It doesn't matter the arm just get an under hook. I am tempted to try to attack the neck, don't it is easy to get swept if I do

Move the trapped knee forward and try to put it on the ground. Then slide my back leg over the body and take side control on the opposite side
# Bottom
Entry: 
- Start from [[Shallow Half Guard|3/4 mount]] or [[Half Guard]]. From here make sure I have control of both elbows (they should be at my sides)
- Shoot the hand that is on the same side as the captured leg.  (near side)
- Get my far side arm punched through and grab onto the back side of their belt (by their but)

### Tips
- Bottom player NEEDS to have their hips at least under their partner. Everything works from not letting the top players leg touch the ground.
- If someone is trying to kamora/americana me, I can use a leg to distarct. While distracted, hide my arm.
- If he stands up go to x guard.


Sweeps. 
- All sweeps from here are off balances. So rock their captured leg back and forth, if I am feeling them being de-based one way or another on the next roll that way bridge and scramble to a better position.
- Homer Simpson sweep - walk legs forward, pull knee around and away from his base, bridge, flip hips (rotating my hips toward the trapped leg), I should end in a double under position
- Waiter sweep - move my front leg up above their knee. Switch my hand from under their thigh to under their ankle (I need to leave the safety of under their leg). rotate my weight forward toward my leg pulling them down (I should end in and over under position)
- Out the back - If they base forward I can get the instep of my back leg on their leg. I can them move my body out the back and kick their leg forward. A scramble to the back should be doable from here.

# Bottom
Defending heel hook - Point toes and keep heel pressed into their body. ALWAYS roll so that me heel is closer to their body, NEVER away

Defending Straight ankle lock - Get my leg away from them, build height

Escape - when there is weakness between their knees try to pull my leg free. (this can be easier when face down)

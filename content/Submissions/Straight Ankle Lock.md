# attacking 
- Start from something like [[Outside Ashi Garami]] 
- I should have an arm around my partners leg. Take that arm and feed it up into my lap. 
- Form a light guillotine or shotgun grip around their foot (with a little bit of play)
- Press my inner foot into their hip so I can create distance and slide until their toes are in my armpit and blade of my wrist is on their achilles.  Once I have that position lock the grip down tight.
- It is very important that I control both sides of their knee to stop their leg from rotating. 
- I should have all my shoulders pulled up so that my arms are as close to my head as possible. Even pulling my chin into my partners ankle helps.
- Pull out the slack by turning my body to the side away from the center line and pressing my feet into their hips. (my butt should be )sticking out. 
- Look away from them when pressing my hips forward (like 1/2 of a scissor jack) to extend their leg
- arch my back to apply pressure directly to the achilles. 

If they are booting consider switching to an [[Heel Hook]] or a [[cross ankle lock]]
# Defence
There are a few main paths of defense. 
- booting - straighten my knee and pull my toes toward me
- just stand up
- get out from their knees

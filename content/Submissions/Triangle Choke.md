# General Advice
If I am bottom and attacking I want to create the triangle with 3 parts. 
1. My calf against the back of their head
2. My thigh against their neck
3. Their arm/shoulder 


# Attacking
To get the arm to the other side I have four options:
- Bridge - If their inside arm is flat on the same side as their shoulder I can bridge and take a two on 1 on that wrist to move it to the other side. Bigger bridge is easier.
- Fist - Place my fist between their shoulder and kneck. Finish the triangle
- Switch legs up. 
	- Cross legs on the other side, 
	- Pull my body to the side where the arm is. 
	- Walk my fingers under their arm
	- Create a gable grip
	- Pull their arm across
	- Reset the triangle
- [[Omoplata]]

Once the arm is across their body it is time to lock in the triangle. This is done by placing my hand on the shin of my leg that is crossed behind their neck. At this point I can set myself up for success by pushing and pulling my quad/hamstring against the side of their head to let out all the slack. I then place my now free leg on the ground or their hip. I use this new leverage to rotate my body tightening the triangle. If they still haven't tapped I an pull their head down, and squeeze.

### Trouble shooting
In open mat [[2025-05-17]] I got and lost the triangle several times. My legs were crossed at the shins and they were able to create distance, shuck the legs and take [[Side control]]. Here is what I think I need to do. 
- The moment I have locked the feet I need to disable the "upper arm" (the arm that is inside the triangle). I do this to break posture. A way to do this is grab the wrist with the arm that is opposite their upper arm. I then use my same side arm to shoot under their arm, and grab my own wrist. I can then press up and move their arm across their body.
- If they have no frames, and I have my legs crossed at the ankles. Pinch at the knees do a crunch to pull their body forward.
- If their arm is across their body and I have their posture broken, notice that their arm is pinched across their body.  Once its pinched grab behind their head with the arm that is same side as both their arms. This locks that arm in place even harder.

# Advice from bottom guard.
If I am coming to this position from [[Closed Guard#Bottom]] then I want to use the knee from the side of the body that is opposite the arm I control and use it to press their arm/shoulder back and put their upper body at an angle. I can then swing my leg over top and grab control at the ankle. From here I can continue the steps above

# Bottom
- Start from butterfly guard bottom
- get a grip behind my partners back, probably double underhooks.
- off balance my partner by pulling them toward and lifting them up with my hooks
- when they base with their hands adjust my grip so it's closer to their head
- let them come down and reset a bit
- repeat this process until their biceps are by their ears
- scissor or stomp sweet with my legs
- come to top and hold for 3 seconds

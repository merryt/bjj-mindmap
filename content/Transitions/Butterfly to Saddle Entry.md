STARTING_POSITION:
  - "[[Butterfly Guard]]"
ENDING_POSITION:
  - "[[The Saddle]]"
---
- Get double grips under the arm pits and push my partner over my head (use butterfly hooks for extra strength)
- Cross one of my arms across the center line and grab a cross armpit grip
- Pulling on my cross armpit grip rotate my body to be perpendicular to my partner
- Keep my farside hook in place, but remove pressure from my nearside hook
- Take my nearside hook and put in it under my farside hook and infront of their shin
- Connect my feet and use the level to pull my partner into [[The Saddle]]

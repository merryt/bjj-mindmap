## General advice
##### Breathing
- Breath is a clue. I listen to my parters breathing. If I notice breathing rate increasing I maintain the position and try to get as efficient as possible.
- I breath through my nose to avoid this.

##### Chest to chest
- When my partner has chest to chest I need to focus on  keeping frames of any sort in and defending the under hooks. If I am starting to lose an underhook I can do a small bridge and as I drop the weight from the bridge I can slide my arm/elbow against my body to reset.

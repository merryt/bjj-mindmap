## Systematically Attacking The Scrimmage: Upper Body Takedowns by Gordon Ryan

Gordon Ryan breaks down upper-body takedowns in this detailed series, teaching high-percentage, overlooked techniques and concepts to boost your standing game at any level of BJJ.

Gordon Ryan is back to address one of the most crucial—and often overlooked—aspects of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu: takedowns

- In this deeply comprehensive 8-volume series, Gordon dives *deep* into upper-body takedowns. This course explores a wide array of high-percentage, underutilized techniques from the standing position, making it an invaluable resource for BJJ practitioners of all levels.
- In this series, Gordon breaks down dozens of the most effective and important upper-body takedowns, going beyond just the mechanics. True to his teaching style, Gordon doesn't just show you how to perform these techniques—he also teaches the essential connections and concepts that allow for immediate implementation.
- Widely regarded as the greatest no-gi competitor of all time, Gordon Ryan has earned his reputation as the undisputed GOAT of BJJ. His techniques work effortlessly at the highest levels of competition, and his approach combines simplicity with fundamentals—allowing him to outclass opponents with ease
- Upper-body takedowns are a cornerstone of the standing game in BJJ. When mastered, they open up a world of opportunities—from guillotines and upper-body joint locks to, of course, positional dominance.

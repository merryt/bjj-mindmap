# Hand to the mat, or touches behind the knees
from 3‍⃣
### Setup
- Both players start standing
- Both players are Active

### Goals
- Get your partners hands to the mat
- Touch the back of their knee
- BONUS GOAL: if you get 3 hands to the ground in a row you can try to connect your hands behind your partners knee.

# Hug the leg
### Setup & Rules
- Both players start standing
- Defender can't down block or sprawl. They can only hand fight

### Goals
- Attacker is trying to get both legs around a defenders legs or hips


# Leg off ground
### Setup & Rules
- Both players start standing and are trying to capture their partners leg and lift it off the mat
### Goal:
- Grab your partners leg, left it off the mat for 3 seconds


# Take down 
### Setup:
- Attacker starts with one of partners legs between theirs
### Goal
- Attacker trys to get their partners back or hands to the ground
- Defender try's to stay up as long as they can


# Rotating positions
from 3‍⃣
### Setup & Rules
- Start from any of these positions
	- front headlock
	- tight waste
	- single
- The attacker can only rotate between positions when the defender has a hand on the ground

### Goals
- Attacker: Get partners hand to the ground and rotate between positions
- Defender: Disconnect and face the attacker


# Single leg recovery
### Setup
- All players have knees on the ground
- Attacker starts with a low single. Their hands are clasped behind their partners leg.  Their head is in the defenders hips (not to the side)

### Goal
- Attacker goal is get the top positions
- Defensive player's goal is to force a turtle

### Tips:
- Attacker should try to wrestle up to standing, or capture both legs and push forward


# Front head lock
### Setup & Rules
- Attacker starts in a front headlock chest to back

### Goals
- Attacker's trys to maintain position and force turtle
- Defender is trying to make it to standing or reverse top/bottom position


# Underhook Basic
### Setup & Rules
- 1 player only has an underhook 
- Underhook-er can only attack the far side leg
- Underhook-ee can only attack the near leg with 

### Goals
- Both players try to take down their partner

### Tips
- Underhook-er should pursue a knee tap. (grab far side knee with their free hand and press their partners upper body)
- Underhook-ee should pursue a uchi mata (with their near side leg they should step inside their partner and press backwards, they should grab their partners hand and toward their hip)

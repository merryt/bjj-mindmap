# Around the elbow and lock hands
From 2‍⃣
### Setup
- Bottom player starts on their side 
- Top player starts with their hands locked under the bottom player

### Goals
- Top players: get around an elbow and lock hands around a shoulder (one underhook + one over hook). End position should be "chest to chest" or "chest to back"
- Bottom players: Get off back

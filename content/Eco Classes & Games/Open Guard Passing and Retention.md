# Getting to hip pocket
### Setup & Rules
- Open guard
- Bottom player is lying on their back, legs up and active. 

### Goals
- bottom player: keep body aligned
- top player: get to j spot/line: get temporary grips on legs

### Tips
- Top player should use 
	- using side steps and crescent steps attempt to step around legs
	- Use Torriando throw bys


# Passing elbows from hip pocket

### Setup & Rules
- Supine Guard 
- Top player has a foot in the hip pocket and a hand on the far side hip

### Goals
- Bottom player: Get to closed guard, open guard, or turtle
- Top player: get Chest to chest or knee on belly
### Tips
- Bottom player
	- Use your nearside knee elbow connection
	- Probe for gaps with knees to recapture gguard
	- Establish an underhoon on the side that is closest to my feet to turn into the top player and rotate to turtle guard
- Top player
	- Establish inside position (lower arm on hip, head on cross body leg)
	- Probe knee elbow connection with my knee to get knee on belly
	- Keep them flat on their back, rotate them around to seporate their knee elbow connection
